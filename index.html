FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about KEYCODE and our AI-powered platform.

How does KEYCODE's AI builder work?

KEYCODE uses a multi-agent AI system powered by DeepSeek, GROQ, HuggingFace, and Ollama. Simply describe your project, and our AI agents generate a complete, production-ready website with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Is my source code protected?

Yes! All generated projects are encrypted with AES-256-GCM encryption. Your source code remains secure until you choose to unlock it. Even we cannot access your encrypted projects without your authorization.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency through our secure Stripe integration. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.

Can I customize the AI-generated website?

Absolutely! You can refine the design through our chat interface, or download the source code and customize it further. We also offer professional customization services for enterprise clients.

How long does it take to get my project?

AI generation takes approximately 3 minutes. After payment, there's a 1-hour security release timer before you can download your source code. This ensures secure delivery and protects both parties.
