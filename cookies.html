What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and design preferences.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (analytics, AI providers) that set their own cookies. These are governed by their respective privacy policies.
