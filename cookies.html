1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Last updated: March 2026

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication - to verify your identity when you log in
  • Preferences - to remember your settings and preferences
  • Analytics - to understand how you use our website
  • Security - to protect your account and data

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly. They cannot be disabled.

Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

Functionality Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services like Stripe (for payments), Google Analytics (for analytics), and our CDN providers.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality. For more information, visit:

  • aboutcookies.org
  • allaboutcookies.org

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
